N5 Life – Safety Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

At N5 Life, we aim to provide a safe and trustworthy environment where customers and service professionals can connect for local services.

1. General Safety

Users should take reasonable precautions when communicating with or meeting another person through N5 Life.

Customers should verify the identity of a service professional before allowing access to their home or property.

2. Worker Verification

Where applicable, N5 Life may collect and review worker information and supporting documents as part of its verification process.

Verification is intended to help establish basic identity and service information. Verification does not guarantee a worker's future conduct, workmanship or service quality.

3. Safe Communication

Users should communicate respectfully and should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information with other users.

4. Home Visit Safety

Customers should use reasonable safety measures when a worker visits their home or property.

Where possible, customers may consider having another trusted person present during a service visit.

5. Suspicious or Unsafe Behaviour

Users should report suspicious, threatening, abusive or unsafe behaviour to N5 Life as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Emergency Situations

N5 Life is not an emergency service. If you are facing an immediate threat to your safety or an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency services immediately.

7. Fraud and Misuse

Fraudulent accounts, fake identities, false documents, scams, impersonation and other misuse of the platform are not permitted.

8. Prohibited Behaviour

Users must not use N5 Life for harassment, threats, violence, exploitation, illegal activities or any behaviour that may put another person at risk.

9. Reporting a Safety Concern

If you experience a safety concern related to the N5 Life platform, please contact us with relevant details so that we can review the matter.

10. Action on Safety Reports

N5 Life may review reported safety concerns and, where appropriate, restrict, suspend or terminate accounts that violate our policies or applicable laws.
